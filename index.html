01 - About

Engineer who likes hard backend problems

I'm a software engineer with a strong backend and distributed-systems focus. Day to day I work in Java & Spring Boot, designing event-driven services on Kafka, taming caching bottlenecks in Redis, and building APIs that hold up under real production load.

At Myntra I build core pieces of the fulfillment and inventory stack, from zone-based order grouping to multi-warehouse stock-transfer pipelines. Before that, at Zee5, I shipped subscription and plan-management microservices for a platform serving 2M+ subscribers.
